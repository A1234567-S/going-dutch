Privacy Policy

Going Dutch

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We don't collect your data. Going Dutch has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run servers that store your information.

Where your trips are kept. Trips, expenses and receipt photos are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. When you invite friends to a trip, Apple's iCloud sharing makes that trip visible to the people you invited. We cannot see it.

Receipts. Receipt photos are read on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition and, on supported iPhones, Apple's on-device language model. Photos are not sent to us or to any third party.

Exchange rates. The app downloads daily exchange rates from api.frankfurter.dev (European Central Bank data) and, for currencies the ECB doesn't cover, open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API). These requests contain only the currency codes needed, not information about you.

Payments. "Pay with UPI" opens your own UPI app with the amount filled in. Going Dutch never sees or handles your payment details.

Notifications. If you allow them, notifications about friends' expenses are created on your iPhone from data already in your iCloud.

Deleting your data. Delete a trip in the app (Trip settings → Delete trip) and it is removed from your iPhone and your iCloud. Leaving a friend's trip removes it from your devices. Deleting the app and its iCloud data (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage) removes everything.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.
